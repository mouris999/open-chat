OpenChat Support & FAQ

What is OpenChat Web?

OpenChat Web is a secure companion desktop application linking to your Android OpenChat phone client. It synchronizes your private chats, groups, and contacts in real time using secure, real-time Firebase databases.

Is it End-to-End Encrypted (E2EE)?

Yes. Private messages use standard Signal Protocol double-ratchet encryption ensuring that only you and the recipient can read what is sent.

How do Disappearing Messages work?

When you enable Disappearing Messages in the Chat Information drawer, all new messages sent by either participant will expire and automatically self-destruct after the chosen timer limit (24 hours or 7 days).

How can I schedule messages?

To schedule a message, long-press the send button on your phone or click the calendar button in the message bar of this Web companion, select your future date/time, and it will be queued until the trigger time.
